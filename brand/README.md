# PrintOk brand kit

The mark is a slanted **P** whose bowl is a sheet of paper with a folded
corner, pushed forward by three ochre speed lines: the initial, the page, and
the pace. It sits in a petrol-ink rounded square.

Everything here is generated by `build.py` from one definition of the mark.
Change the constants there and run `python3 brand/build.py`. Do not hand-edit
the outputs. The build also refreshes the site's favicon, touch icon, share
image and the Windows agent's icon, so the product always matches the kit.

## Logo

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `logo/printok-logo.svg` | Default. Horizontal lockup on light backgrounds |
| `logo/printok-logo-on-dark.svg` | The same on petrol or other dark backgrounds |
| `logo/printok-logo-stacked.svg` | Square spaces: splash screens, stickers, social avatars |
| `logo/printok-mark.svg` | The mark alone: favicons, app icons, small spaces |
| `logo/printok-wordmark*.svg` | Where the mark already appears nearby |
| `logo/*-mono-*.svg`, `printok-mark-ink/white.svg` | One colour only: stamps, engraving, fax, receipts |

The wordmark is outlined, so the SVGs render the same with or without the
font installed.

**Clear space:** keep a margin of at least the height of the P's bowl (a
quarter of the mark's height) on every side.

**Minimum size:** the mark at 16 px, and the horizontal lockup at 96 px wide.
Below 96 px, use the mark alone.

**Don't** stretch it, recolour the P, remove or reorder the speed lines, set
the wordmark in another font, or put the colour logo on a busy photo. For
those cases use the mono white version on a darkened area.

## Icons

`icons/icons.svg` is the site's own sprite, with line icons from
[Lucide](https://lucide.dev) (ISC license). Use them as
`<svg class="icon"><use href="/icons.svg#printer"/></svg>`. They take the
colour and size of the surrounding text. **No emoji in the interface.**

## App icons and social

`app-icons/` has PNGs from 16 to 512 px, `apple-touch-icon.png` (180),
`favicon.ico` (16/32/48) and a maskable 512 px icon with the mark inside
Android's safe zone. `social/printok-og.png` is the 1200×630 share card.
