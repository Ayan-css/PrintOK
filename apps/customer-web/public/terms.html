We do not print anything. The printing is performed by the shop you chose, on its own equipment, using its own paper and consumables. The contract for the printing itself is between you and that shop. We are not a party to it and we do not control the quality, timing or outcome of the printing.

3.2 Price and payment

  • The shop sets its own rates per page, by colour, single or double sided, and paper size. The total is calculated from the shop's current rate card and shown to you in full before you pay. There are no fees added at the counter.
  • The page count is verified on our servers from the document itself, so you are charged for the pages actually in the file.
  • You may pay online by UPI, card, net banking or wallet through Razorpay, or in cash at the counter where the shop offers it.
  • Online payments below ₹1 cannot be processed by the gateway; pay at the counter instead.
  • A job is only sent to the printer once payment is confirmed, or the shop approves it.

3.3 Collecting your printout

You will be given a token number. Show it at the shop counter to collect. Collect on the same day unless the shop agrees otherwise — an uncollected printout may be disposed of, and is not refundable once printed.

4.3 How you are paid

  • Cash taken at your counter is yours in full; no service fee applies to it.
  • Direct settlement requires you to complete Razorpay's KYC. Until Razorpay marks your account active, nothing can be split to it.
