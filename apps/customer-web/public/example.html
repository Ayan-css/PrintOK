01

Where the file goes

Three pieces, and only one of them sits in your shop.

01.1

The customer's phone

They scan the QR on your counter, pick copies, colour and pages, and see the price before paying a rupee. No app, no account.

  • No app to install
  • Pays by UPI or card
  • Gets a token number
01.2

PrintOk

Counts the pages from the file itself, prices the job from your rates, takes the payment and holds the queue until your printer is ready for it.

  • Pages verified server-side
  • Payment and printing kept separate
  • File deleted when done
01.3

Your shop PC

A small program collects paid jobs and sends them to the printer you already own. It pairs once and reconnects on its own after a restart.

  • Pairs once with a code
  • Reconnects after a restart
  • Removes files after printing
02

What it costs you

A platform fee on paid orders, and nothing on cash taken at your counter. Razorpay's payment processing charges are separate, are deducted before settlement, and are not absorbed by PrintOk.

Plan Monthly Platform fee Orders / mo Printers
Free Free 2.0% 100 1
Starter ₹149 1.0% 1,000 2
Business ₹349 0.5% 4,000 5
Pro ₹699 0% 10,000 10

On a ₹100 order on Business you receive ₹97.14 — the order less the ₹0.50 platform fee and Razorpay's ₹2.36. On Free the same order returns ₹95.64, the platform fee being ₹2.00. Even on Pro, where PrintOk's fee is nil, Razorpay's ₹2.36 still applies.
