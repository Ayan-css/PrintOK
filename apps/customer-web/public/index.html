How it works

Three things to set up once, then it runs itself.

🏬

1. List your shop

Register in a couple of minutes. You get a QR poster for your counter and a dashboard to watch the queue.

💻

2. Connect your PC

Download the print agent, enter a pairing code from your dashboard, and your existing printer is online. It reconnects on its own after a restart.

🖨️

3. Jobs just arrive

Paid jobs print automatically. You only step in when something actually needs you — paper out, or a job that needs a decision.

Built around how a shop actually runs

💸 You set your own rates

Per page, black & white or colour, single or double sided, A4 or A3. Change them whenever you like — jobs already quoted keep their original price.

⚡ No counter explaining

The customer picks copies, colour and page range themselves. You see a token number and a finished printout.

🔒 Documents don't linger

Files are deleted once the job is done. Nothing sits on your PC, and there's a record of every deletion.

🧾 Money you can reconcile

Payment and printing are tracked separately, so a payment that didn't print is visible rather than silently lost.

🛟 Failures surface

If a PC dies mid-job, the job comes back to the queue — unless it might already have printed, in which case you're asked instead of paper being wasted.

🔑 Every PC is revocable

Each connected PC gets its own key. Remove one without disturbing the others.

Questions

Do I need to buy a new printer?

No. PrintOk works with the printer you already have, through a small program on any Windows PC connected to it.

What happens if my PC or internet goes down?

Jobs wait in the queue. When the PC comes back it reconnects and picks them up. A job that was mid-print when the PC died is held for you to confirm rather than reprinted automatically.

What file types can customers send?

PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, PowerPoint and images. Page count is verified on our side, so the price reflects what actually prints.

How do customers pay?

On their own phone, before printing. You can also approve a job manually if someone pays cash at the counter.

What happens to customer documents?

They're removed once the job reaches a final state, both from our storage and from your PC.

Can I use more than one printer?

Yes. Each printer gets its own QR code and its own queue.
