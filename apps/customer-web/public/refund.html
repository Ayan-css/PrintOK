Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026 · Forms part of the Terms & Conditions.

The rule of thumb. Before it prints, you can cancel and get your money back. Once paper and toner have been used, it cannot be un-printed — but if the printing went wrong, you are refunded or reprinted.

1. Cancelling before printing

A print job can be cancelled at no cost at any time before it reaches the printer — while it is awaiting payment, or queued and waiting for the shop's agent. If you paid online, the full amount is refunded.

Closing the payment window before paying cancels nothing and costs nothing: the job simply stays unpaid and is never sent to print.

2. Once printing has started

Printing consumes paper and toner that cannot be recovered, so an order that has printed correctly, to the settings you chose, is not refundable simply because you changed your mind.

3. When you are refunded anyway

A full refund is due where:

  • you paid but the job never printed;
  • the shop refused, cancelled or could not fulfil the job;
  • the printout does not match the settings you paid for — printed in black and white when you paid for colour, single sided when you paid for double, wrong page range, wrong paper size, wrong number of copies;
  • the printout is unusable through a fault at the shop — blank or streaked pages, jammed or damaged paper, toner failure;
  • you were charged twice for the same job;
  • you were charged an amount other than the total shown before you paid.

A partial refund applies where only some of the order was affected — for example, three of ten copies unusable means those three are refunded.

At the shop's discretion, a reprint may be offered instead of a refund. You are not obliged to accept it.

4. When a refund is not due

  • The job printed correctly and you changed your mind.
  • The document itself was wrong — wrong file, wrong version, a typo, formatting that came out as the file specified. The preview shows the pages before you pay, so check it.
  • You chose the wrong settings and the job printed as chosen.
  • You did not collect the printout.

5. How to ask

Speak to the shop first. They have the printout and can usually resolve it on the spot with a reprint or a cash refund.

If that does not resolve it, contact us within 7 days of the order with your token number and a short description of the problem, through the contact form. A photo of the printout helps.

6. How long it takes

  • We acknowledge a refund request within 48 hours.
  • We decide within 7 working days, after checking with the shop.
  • An approved refund is initiated through Razorpay within 2 working days of the decision.
  • The money reaches you in 5–7 working days from then, depending on your bank or UPI provider. That leg is outside our control.

Refunds go back to the original payment method. We cannot redirect a refund to a different account, card or UPI ID.

7. Cash payments

Cash paid at the counter is settled by the shop directly. Ask the shop for a cash refund — we never received that money and cannot return it. We will still help mediate if the shop does not resolve it.

8. Shop subscription plans

Monthly plan fees are charged in advance and are not refundable for a partial month. Cancel at any time and the plan runs to the end of the paid period, after which it is not renewed. We have no lock-in and no cancellation fee.

If we ever suspend or remove a shop for a reason that is not its fault, the unused part of its plan fee is refunded pro rata.

9. If you are still not satisfied

Escalate to the Grievance Officer named in our Privacy Policy. You may also use the National Consumer Helpline (1915) or e-Daakhil at edaakhil.nic.in. Your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 are unaffected by this policy.
