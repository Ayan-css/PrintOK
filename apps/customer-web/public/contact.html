How PrintOk works. PrintOk is an ordering and payment platform. The print shop you order from is an independent business: it prints your order, hands it over and sets its own prices. PrintOk takes the order and the payment and passes the job to that shop.

About a print order

Speak to the shop first. Its name is shown at the top of the ordering page and on your confirmation, and it has your printout — most problems are fixed on the spot with a reprint.

If that does not resolve it, email PrintOk support with your order reference (shown on your confirmation screen) and a short description. We acknowledge within 48 hours. Refunds are handled as set out in the Cancellation & Refund Policy.
